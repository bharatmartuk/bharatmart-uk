Embrace the spiritual warmth of traditional Indian festivals with our hand-painted floral terracotta diyas. Each lamp is meticulously crafted by master artisans in Jaipur, using locally sourced eco-friendly clay. The intricate floral motifs are painted by hand, ensuring that no two pieces are exactly alike.

These diyas are designed to hold oil or wax for long-lasting illumination during Diwali, Karthigai Deepam, or your daily puja rituals. Their timeless aesthetic bridges the gap between heritage and modern home decor, making them perfect as gifts or festive highlights.

  • Dimensions: 3 inches diameter, 1.2 inches height
